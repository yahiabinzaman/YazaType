"Most modern software suffers from a divide between visual craftsmanship and system-level engineering. Having spent 8+ years directing prepress manufacturing and industrial ERP at ColorLab, I engineered YazaType to permanently eliminate broken Bengali typography."

Bengali computer typography has been hindered for decades by awkward vowel reordering, broken Adobe Illustrator/InDesign conjuncts, and input lag. As both a software engineer and prepress design director, Yahia Bin Zaman designed YazaType from scratch as a zero-latency native engine with dual Unicode and ANSI engines running simultaneously in real time.

Having spent over eight years directing enterprise brand identity, high-volume prepress packaging, and industrial ERP operations at ColorLab, Yahia operates at the intersection of engineering and visual craft. His work spans native desktop software in Swift and C++, production web architectures in Next.js and TypeScript, and intelligent AI agent pipelines.
